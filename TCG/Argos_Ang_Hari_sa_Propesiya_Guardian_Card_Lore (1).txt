ARGOS: ANG HARI SA PROPESIYA
THE SIX GUARDIANS + ARGOS & LUNA
TCG CARD LORE AND STATUS


I. ALDRIN DEL ROSARIO
FIRE GUARDIAN
Blessed by: Gugurang — Bathala ng Apoy
Sacred Weapon: Sibat ni Panganoron

ATK: 18
DEF: 16
LIFE: 28

LORE:
Aldrin Del Rosario was chosen by Gugurang to carry the flame that can either protect the world or reduce it to ashes. Within his body burns the Apoy ng Panganoron, a divine flame said to have been born from the heart of an ancient volcano.

Aldrin is not merely a warrior of fire. He is the Guardian of willpower, courage, and destruction. The hotter his resolve burns, the stronger Panganoron becomes.

But Gugurang gave him one warning:

"Ang apoy na hindi kayang kontrolin ng puso ay apoy na sisira sa sariling tahanan."

When the Kalibata awakens, Aldrin must decide whether his sacred flame will become the world's salvation—or its final inferno.

SPECIAL ATTACK:
PANGANORON: HUKOM NG APOY
Aldrin raises the Sibat ni Panganoron toward the heavens. A colossal pillar of divine fire descends upon his enemy.

Effect: Deal +8 damage to one enemy. If the enemy is already damaged, the attack becomes +10 damage instead.


II. ACE GARCIA
WIND / AIR GUARDIAN
Blessed by: Sibol — Bathala ng Hangin
Sacred Weapons: Habagat & Amihan

ATK: 16
DEF: 14
LIFE: 25

LORE:
Ace Garcia is the Guardian whom no enemy can truly catch.

Blessed by Sibol, Ace possesses dominion over the two great winds: Habagat, the furious southern wind, and Amihan, the cold and gentle breath of the north.

His twin Ringblades are more than weapons. Each blade contains the spirit of one wind. Habagat grants overwhelming force, while Amihan grants impossible speed.

Ace fights without standing still.

To his enemies, there is never one Ace.

There are hundreds.

And by the time they realize which one is real, the battle is already over.

SPECIAL ATTACK:
DALAWANG HANGIN: BAGYONG WALANG HANGGAN
Ace throws Habagat and Amihan in opposite directions. The two Ringblades create a spiraling storm that closes around the enemy.

Effect: Deal 7 damage and reduce the enemy's DEF by 5 for the next turn.


III. DANIEL MADRIGAL
EARTH GUARDIAN
Blessed by: Taliyak — Bathala ng Lupa
Sacred Armor: Baluti ni Carpio

ATK: 12
DEF: 28
LIFE: 38

LORE:
Daniel Madrigal does not need a weapon.

He is the fortress.

Chosen by Taliyak, Daniel carries the ancient Baluti ni Carpio, a divine armor connected to the bones and foundations of the earth itself.

Every strike against the armor feeds its power.

Every step Daniel takes makes the ground remember who its Guardian is.

He is the slowest of the six Guardians—but once Daniel takes his position, moving him becomes almost impossible.

The ancient prophecy calls him:

"Ang Pader na Hindi Guguho."

When the other Guardians fall, Daniel remains.

When the battlefield is destroyed, Daniel stands upon its ruins.

SPECIAL ATTACK:
PUSO NG LUPA
Daniel drives his fist into the ground, causing enormous stone pillars to erupt beneath his enemies.

Effect: Deal 6 damage and gain +8 DEF until the beginning of your next turn.


IV. JESSIE DANAO
WATER GUARDIAN
Blessed by: Amanikable — Bathala ng Karagatan
Sacred Weapons: Alon & Daluyong

ATK: 15
DEF: 19
LIFE: 32

LORE:
Jessie Danao carries two mysterious orbs known as Alon and Daluyong.

The blue orb, Alon, represents the calmness of the sea—the healing waters, gentle rain, and endless cycle of life.

The red orb, Daluyong, represents the wrath of the ocean—the storm, the tsunami, and the fury of Amanikable.

Jessie alone can command both.

His greatest strength is not the ability to destroy.

It is the ability to change.

Water can become a shield.

A weapon.

A storm.

A healer.

Or an unstoppable wave.

The ancient texts describe Jessie as:

"Ang Tahimik na Dagat bago ang Daluyong."

SPECIAL ATTACK:
ALON AT DALUYONG: GALIT NG KARAGATAN
Jessie combines the two orbs. A massive tidal serpent emerges and crashes upon the enemy.

Effect: Deal 8 damage. If Jessie has lost LIFE, restore 4 LIFE after the attack.


V. ALICE BELMONTE
LIGHT GUARDIAN
Blessed by: Mayari — Bathala ng Liwanag at Buwan
Sacred Weapon: Sinag — Moon Staff

ATK: 14
DEF: 21
LIFE: 30

LORE:
Alice Belmonte was chosen by Mayari to become the bearer of Sinag, the Moon Staff.

Unlike ordinary light, Alice's power does not burn.

It reveals.

It exposes the darkness hidden within the hearts of men and illuminates paths where none appear to exist.

Sinag draws its strength from the moon, changing with the phases of the celestial body.

During the full moon, Alice becomes one of the most powerful Guardians alive.

But the true purpose of her blessing is not destruction.

She was chosen to guide the Argos.

Where Raven walks into darkness, Alice is the light that ensures he does not lose himself.

SPECIAL ATTACK:
SINAG: LIWANAG NG KABILUGAN
Alice raises Sinag and summons the full moon above the battlefield. A beam of divine lunar light strikes the enemy while surrounding her allies.

Effect: Deal 6 damage and restore 5 LIFE to an allied Guardian.


VI. DERRICK BAUTISTA
DARK GUARDIAN
Blessed by: Sakulag — Bathala ng Dilim, Lason at Takot
Sacred Weapon: Pangil ng Pitong Lason

ATK: 19
DEF: 12
LIFE: 23

LORE:
Derrick Bautista is the Guardian whom even the other Guardians fear.

Blessed by Sakulag, he carries Pangil ng Pitong Lason, a divine dagger containing seven supernatural poisons.

Each poison attacks something different.

The flesh.

The blood.

The mind.

The senses.

The soul.

And the deepest fears of its victim.

Derrick does not fight like a warrior.

He hunts.

He waits in the shadows.

He strikes once.

And then he disappears.

The blessing of Sakulag comes with a terrible price: the more Derrick uses the power of darkness, the more the darkness begins to recognize him as one of its own.

SPECIAL ATTACK:
PANGIL NG PITONG LASON
Derrick disappears into the shadows before striking the enemy with all seven poisons.

Effect: Deal 7 damage and inflict Poison. The poisoned enemy loses 2 LIFE at the beginning of its next two turns.


VII. RAVEN CRUZ
THE ARGOS — HARI SA PROPESIYA
Role: The Chosen King
Sacred Title: Argos
Weapon: The Eye of Argos / Divine Power of the Prophecy

ATK: 20
DEF: 24
LIFE: 40

LORE:
Raven Cruz was never supposed to become a king.

He was simply a young man living an ordinary life—until the awakening of the mysterious eye changed everything.

The ancient prophecy speaks of a being known as the Argos.

Not a god.

Not a Guardian.

Not a servant of the old powers.

But the one who stands above them.

Raven's awakening connects him to a power older than the six blessings. Through the Eye of Argos, he can see fragments of the past, possible futures, and the hidden truth behind the coming Kalibata.

The six Guardians were blessed by the Bathala.

Raven was chosen by something even the Bathala fear.

His greatest battle will not be against Kalibata.

It will be against the question:

"If he possesses the power to become a god, will he remain human?"

SPECIAL ATTACK:
ARGOS: PANINGIN NG TADHANA
Raven opens the Eye of Argos and sees the enemy's next move before it happens.

Effect: Raven deals 10 damage and ignores 5 DEF from the target.

PASSIVE — HARI SA PROPESIYA:
Once per battle, when Raven would be defeated, he remains at 1 LIFE instead.


VIII. AQUILA
THE LUNA
Role: The Moon / Keeper of the Argos
Sacred Title: Luna
Connection: Raven's destined counterpart

ATK: 15
DEF: 23
LIFE: 36

LORE:
Aquila is the Luna—the one destined to walk beside the Argos.

Where Raven represents the will to change destiny, Aquila represents the force that keeps that destiny from consuming him.

Her connection to the moon allows her to sense the movements of the supernatural world long before the Guardians themselves recognize the danger.

She is neither servant nor subordinate to Raven.

She is his balance.

When Raven becomes consumed by the power of Argos, Aquila is the one capable of calling him back.

The ancient prophecy contains a forgotten verse:

"Kapag nagising ang mata ng hari, hahanapin niya ang liwanag ng buwan."

For without the Luna, the Argos may become powerful enough to save the world—but lose himself in the process.

SPECIAL ATTACK:
LUNA: HALIK NG BUWAN
Aquila summons the power of the moon and surrounds an ally with a silver lunar barrier.

Effect: Restore 8 LIFE to one allied character and give that character +5 DEF until the next turn.

PASSIVE — LIWANAG NG LUNA:
When Raven is on the battlefield, Aquila gains +3 ATK and +3 DEF.
